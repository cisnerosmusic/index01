[ note ] Studio news · 2026-07-24

Index01 LLC: the studio puts down roots in Florida.

On July 24, 2026 we filed the Articles of Organization of Index01 LLC with the state of Florida's business registry, and the state completed the registration: the company exists. It's paperwork, yes. But for us it's the same idea we propose to every client, applied at home: what's built seriously is built to last.

[01] Why it matters

Practicing what we preach.

For months we've been telling every business that a serious presence starts with a serious structure: pages of your own, clear data, rules in writing. Formalizing the studio as a Florida company is that same principle, taken to paper. The name you see on this site is now also a name inscribed in the state's public registry.

01 / Commitment

We're here to stay

You don't incorporate an LLC for a passing project. It's the formal signal that Index01 operates, invoices, and answers as a company, with the rules of the state of Florida behind it.

02 / Clarity

Clean accounts

Contracts and invoices in the company's name, a clean separation between the studio and the personal, and a public registry where anyone can verify we exist. The transparency we ask for, offered.

03 / Continuity

Nothing changes for you

If you already work with us, everything stays the same: same prices, same agreements, same treatment. New documents, contracts and invoices, now come out under Index01 LLC. That's all.

[ + ] The exact fact

INDEX01 LLC is now incorporated with the Florida Division of Corporations (Sunbiz): Articles of Organization filed July 24, 2026, document L26000393420, verifiable in Sunbiz's public registry. We promised to update this note once the registration was complete, and here it is. That's how we tell things: with dates and verifiable facts, also when we talk about ourselves.

[02] The philosophy behind the paperwork

The small is built the same way as the big.

At Index01 we believe beauty is a consequence of intention, and that idea doesn't live only in design. A company is built the way we build a website: doors, not walls. Everything in its place, with a clear name and a readable record, for people, for machines, and now for institutions too. Being the answer, not the tenth link, starts with existing without ambiguity wherever the serious questions are asked. The LLC is exactly that: structured data, but before the state of Florida. How we think, in full, is in About the studio.
