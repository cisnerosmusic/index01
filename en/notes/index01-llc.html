[ note ] Studio news · 2026-07-24

Index01 LLC: the studio puts down roots in Florida.

On July 24, 2026 we filed the formation of Index01 LLC with the state of Florida's business registry. It's paperwork, yes. But for us it's the same idea we propose to every client, applied at home: what's built seriously is built to last.

[01] Why it matters

Practicing what we preach.

For months we've been telling every business that a serious presence starts with a serious structure: pages of your own, clear data, rules in writing. Formalizing the studio as a Florida company is that same principle, taken to paper. The name you see on this site becomes a name inscribed in the state's public registry as well.

01 / Commitment

We're here to stay

You don't file an LLC for a passing project. It's the formal signal that Index01 operates, invoices, and answers as a company, with the rules of the state of Florida behind it.

02 / Clarity

Clean accounts

Contracts and invoices in the company's name, a clean separation between the studio and the personal, and a public registry where anyone can verify we exist. The transparency we ask for, offered.

03 / Continuity

Nothing changes for you

If you already work with us, everything stays the same: same prices, same agreements, same treatment. Once the state completes the registration, new documents will come out under Index01 LLC. That's all.

[ + ] The exact fact

Formation of Index01 LLC filed on July 24, 2026 with the Florida Division of Corporations (Sunbiz), pending the state's normal processing. When the registration is complete, this note will be updated. That's how we tell things here: with dates and verifiable facts, also when we talk about ourselves.
