[01] How it's built

Three pieces working together.

01 / Data

Structured data

Schema.org is the language for telling a machine who you are, what you do, and where you are, without ambiguity. We write it complete and by hand for every page: the business, the services, the FAQs, the breadcrumbs.

Schema.org
02 / Access

Doors open to AI

An llms.txt file that summarizes your site for assistants, and a robots.txt that explicitly welcomes their crawlers: GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended. If you want to be cited, first they have to be able to read you.

llms.txt
03 / Shape

Citable answers

AIs cite clean answers, not walls of text. So each page answers one concrete question, with a clear title and direct content. It's our doors-not-walls architecture, applied to the answer layer.

Doors, not walls
[ + ] Freshness matters

These systems tend to cite what was updated recently. A site that sits still loses ground on its own, even if it's well built. That's why AEO isn't a one-time job: it's sustained with ongoing maintenance that keeps your data alive and your site citable month after month.

[03] Questions about this service

What people usually ask about AEO.

What is AEO?

Answer Engine Optimization: preparing your site so AI assistants and Google's automatic answer can read it, understand it, and cite it as a source when someone asks about a service or a business like yours.

How is it different from SEO?

SEO works to get you into a list so someone picks you. AEO works to make you the single answer the assistant delivers, with your name as the source. They share foundations; AEO adds structured data, llms.txt, and citable content.

How do you get cited?

Structured data that removes ambiguity, pages that answer concrete questions, and constant freshness. There's no trick or shortcut: it's building the site in the language machines understand and keeping it alive.
