[03] Questions about this service

What people usually ask about measurement.

What is remarketing?

Showing your business again to someone who already visited. Most people don't buy or write the first time; with pixels properly installed you can remind them you exist right when they're deciding. Without measurement, those people leave and don't come back.

Is measuring visitors legal?

Yes, with the consent the law requires: a clear notice where the visitor accepts or declines before anything activates. We install it that way by default. That protects you, which is what many overlook until it becomes a problem.

Do I need it?

If you invest or plan to invest in advertising, yes: measurement tells you what works and remarketing multiplies the result. If you don't run campaigns yet, it can wait. We tell you straight when we read your case.
