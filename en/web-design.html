[03] Questions about this service

What people usually ask about design.

Why no WordPress or templates?

Because a template is another business's face placed over yours, and because WordPress and its plugins add weight, maintenance, and security doors a custom site doesn't need. We write the code ourselves: it loads faster, depends on no licenses, and it's yours.

Is it more expensive than a template?

It costs more than buying a template, but compare what matters: a generic template doesn't rank the same, loads slower, and doesn't look like your business. Our FAQ publishes reference ranges, upfront and without mystery.

Can I update it later?

Yes. The site is delivered with complete, documented code, and it's yours once the project is settled. Edit it yourself, hand it to someone else, or leave it under our ongoing maintenance, which also keeps it fresh for search engines and AI assistants.
