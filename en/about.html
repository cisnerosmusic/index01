[ about ] The studio

Beauty is a consequence of intention.

Index01 is a web design, SEO, and AEO studio based in Miami, Florida, serving clients anywhere, remotely, in Spanish and English. We work from the code up and build sites designed to be the answer that search engines and AI assistants give. This page doesn't tell you what we do; that's in services. It tells you how we think.

[01] The sensibility

We don't have an aesthetic. We have judgment.

We don't conceive a website as a collection of effects, but as a sequence of decisions. Every project has its own voice: our job is not to impose an aesthetic, but to find the rhythm that already exists in the nature of each business and make it visible through code. That's why we don't aim for our pages to look alike; we aim for all of them to feel right.

01 / Intention

Nothing moves just because

Every transition, every appearance, and every state change exists because it serves a function. Animation is never an ornament: it's language. What doesn't contribute doesn't get in, however pretty it is.

02 / Respect

Attention is a privilege

A visitor's attention isn't captured: it's earned. A fast page is a form of respect and an unnecessary effect is an interruption. That's why our sites weigh little and respond instantly.

03 / Breathing

The interface must breathe

Not everything should happen at once. Pause produces clarity, emptiness is part of the design, and things reveal themselves instead of jumping at you. People don't remember a transition; they remember how they felt.

[ + ] The signature

The true signature of an experience is not in the shape of things. It's in the way they happen.

[02] The technique

We work from the code up, and that's not negotiable.

We don't pursue dependency when understanding is possible. We prefer light, clear, maintainable systems, where every component can be explained and every decision respects performance.

A

Our own code

No WordPress, no Wix, no plugins or limiting platforms. HTML, CSS, and JavaScript written for each project. Fewer borrowed parts, fewer things that break, and a site that's truly yours.

B

Zero dead weight

Every effect must justify its cost and every request to the browser must justify its existence. And all motion respects accessibility preferences: our sites work perfectly without any animation layer at all.

C

SEO and AEO in every decision

Structured data, sitemap, llms.txt, and a separate-page architecture are not extras added at the end: they're the standard every project is born with, so people find you and machines cite you.

[03] Who's behind it
