[03] Questions about this service

What people usually ask about SEO.

What is technical SEO?

The part of ranking you don't see: fast loading, flawless phone behavior, separate-page structure, and the signals Google actually measures, like Core Web Vitals. Without these foundations, content and advertising are built on sand.

Do you guarantee position one?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. We deliver the site technically ready to be found and cited from day one; authority is built over time. It's in writing in our legal notice, because we prefer an informed client to an empty promise.

What are Core Web Vitals?

The metrics Google uses to measure a page's real experience: how long the important content takes to appear, how fast it responds to a tap, and how much it shifts while loading. A site born light passes them comfortably.
